# Dawati Invitation Template Collection: Design Specifications

This document outlines the 12 distinct art directions for the Dawati digital wedding invitation collection. Each template is designed to be high-fidelity, premium, and functional as a dynamic system.

## 01 — Ivory Editorial
- **Background**: Warm Ivory (`#fcf9f8`)
- **Typography**: Large, central Arabic display font for "سارة & محمد" in Deep Charcoal (`#2d2d2d`). 
- **Layout**: Minimalist, magazine-style layout with generous whitespace.
- **Accents**: Extremely thin champagne gold divider lines (`#e5d3b3`).
- **Dynamic Areas**: Guest name at the top; Date/Time/Location in a small, refined block at the bottom left; QR and Countdown balanced on the bottom right.

## 02 — Midnight Luxury
- **Background**: Deep Charcoal / Matte Black (`#1a1a1a`)
- **Typography**: Warm Ivory text for high contrast.
- **Layout**: Center-aligned, dramatic vertical composition.
- **Accents**: Subtle champagne gold border or "glow" around the couple's names.
- **Dynamic Areas**: Central focal point for names; all other details in smaller, elegant type below.

## 03 — Soft Champagne
- **Background**: Cream to Champagne gradient (`#f6f3f2` to `#e5d3b3`).
- **Typography**: Muted Taupe (`#8a8a8a`).
- **Layout**: Soft, organic layered shapes (curved lines) in the background.
- **Dynamic Areas**: Integrated into the "flow" of the background curves.

## 04 — Modern Arabic
- **Background**: Pure Warm Ivory.
- **Typography**: Bold, contemporary Arabic calligraphic style for names.
- **Layout**: Asymmetric, focus on typographic weight.
- **Accents**: Geometric accents inspired by modern Riyadh architecture.

## 05 — Architectural
- **Background**: Neutral Stone/Beige.
- **Typography**: Structured, serif for details; custom fluid calligraphy for names.
- **Layout**: Symmetrical, framed by architectural geometric lines.
- **Accents**: Balanced gold linework.

## 06 — Soft Rose
- **Background**: Very subtle Dusty Rose (`#f4ecea`).
- **Typography**: Deep Espresso.
- **Layout**: Centered, with minimal, abstract organic shapes in the corners.
- **Accents**: Ivory overlays.

## 07 — Black & Ivory
- **Background**: High-contrast split or solid Charcoal.
- **Typography**: Bold Ivory Editorial style.
- **Layout**: High-fashion house aesthetic, zero "wedding" clichés.
- **Accents**: No gold; pure monochromatic luxury.

## 08 — Monogram
- **Background**: Soft Ivory.
- **Typography**: Central monogram "س. م" in large, transparent-outline style in the background.
- **Layout**: Names overlaid on the large monogram.
- **Accents**: Subtle champagne texture.

## 09 — Layered Card
- **Background**: Faux-texture Ivory cardstock with drop shadows.
- **Typography**: Embossed/Debossed effect appearance.
- **Layout**: Multiple "cards" appearing to overlap.
- **Accents**: Champagne gold "wax seal" icon near the guest name.

## 10 — Minimal Frame
- **Background**: Clean Ivory.
- **Typography**: Small, elegant names at the center.
- **Layout**: A single, ultra-thin 1px champagne frame with 10% padding.
- **Accents**: The frame itself is the only decorative element.

## 11 — Contemporary Gulf
- **Background**: Warm Beige.
- **Typography**: Traditional Kufic-inspired but modern.
- **Layout**: Pattern-heavy background using subtle 5% opacity Sadu or Mashrabiya patterns.
- **Accents**: Charcoal typography for clarity.

## 12 — Signature Luxury
- **Background**: Deep Espresso / Dark Brown.
- **Typography**: Premium Gold/Champagne.
- **Layout**: Full-screen "Hero" style with the names taking 50% of the vertical space.
- **Accents**: Subtle grain/noise texture for a paper-like feel.
- **Dynamic Areas**: Minimalist icons for Date/Location.
