# Dawati "Palace & Architecture" Invitation Collection: Design Specifications (2026)

This new collection moves away from traditional "card" aesthetics toward an immersive, environmental, and architectural visual language. Each of the 15 designs is inspired by the atmosphere of a luxury Saudi wedding venue.

## Shared Visual Language
- **Materials**: Ivory stone, polished marble, brushed champagne gold, deep espresso wood, velvet, and silk.
- **Lighting**: Dramatic light and shadow, soft chandelier glows, architectural backlighting.
- **Typography**: Large-scale, editorial Arabic display fonts for the couple's names.
- **Philosophy**: The invitation is a "venue entrance" rather than a piece of stationery.

---

## 01 — THE PALACE ARCH
- **Concept**: A massive, sculptural architectural arch inspired by a grand palace entrance.
- **Visuals**: Deep perspective, ivory stone texture, warm backlighting within the arch.
- **Typography**: Names centered within the archway.

## 02 — GRAND FOYER
- **Concept**: The atmosphere of a luxury ballroom foyer.
- **Visuals**: Tall minimalist columns, a soft chandelier glow from the top, marble floor reflections, deep shadows.
- **Typography**: Vertical editorial layout.

## 03 — COUTURE
- **Concept**: High-fashion campaign aesthetic.
- **Visuals**: High contrast (Black & Warm White), massive negative space, radical cropping.
- **Typography**: Oversized, bold Arabic typography taking up 60% of the screen.

## 04 — SCULPTURAL SILK
- **Concept**: A massive installation of flowing silk or velvet.
- **Visuals**: Organic, architectural folds of fabric in Muted Mocha or Burgundy.
- **Typography**: Floating elegantly over the fabric folds.

## 05 — MARBLE MONOLITH
- **Concept**: A solid slab of premium stone.
- **Visuals**: Deep Espresso marble with gold veins. The text is "carved" into the stone.
- **Typography**: Engraved/Debossed effect with gold leaf fills.

## 06 — THE CHANDELIER
- **Concept**: Abstract play of light and crystal.
- **Visuals**: Out-of-focus crystal bokeh and champagne metal lines.
- **Typography**: Light, thin, and glowing.

## 07 — DESERT ARCHITECTURE
- **Concept**: Modern Riyadh architectural minimalism.
- **Visuals**: Warm beige stone, sharp geometric shadows, clean linear compositions.
- **Typography**: Modern, structured Kufic-inspired display.

## 08 — VELVET DRAMA
- **Concept**: A theatre/stage entrance.
- **Visuals**: Deep charcoal velvet background with a single "spotlight" on the names.
- **Typography**: Centered, high-contrast ivory.

## 09 — THE GATEWAY
- **Concept**: A modern interpretation of a traditional gate.
- **Visuals**: Interlocking champagne metal bars forming a geometric frame.
- **Typography**: Integrated into the metalwork.

## 10 — IVORY GARDEN (MODERN)
- **Concept**: A sculptural floral installation (no generic flowers).
- **Visuals**: Abstract, 3D-rendered white porcelain flowers or stone-carved petals.
- **Typography**: Minimalist and airy.

## 11 — REFLECTION
- **Concept**: Luxury hotel lobby aesthetic.
- **Visuals**: Dark polished surfaces with reflected warm lights.
- **Typography**: Mirrored or dual-layered typography.

## 12 — THE STAGE
- **Concept**: A focus on the wedding 'Kosha' or stage.
- **Visuals**: Layered backdrop panels in different textures (Silk/Stone).
- **Typography**: Placed as the "headliner" of the stage.

## 13 — SMOKED GLASS
- **Concept**: Ultra-modern tech luxury.
- **Visuals**: Semi-transparent charcoal glass overlays with champagne gold borders.
- **Typography**: Crisp, sharp, and high-definition.

## 14 — EARTHEN LUXE
- **Concept**: Organic but refined.
- **Visuals**: Terracotta and Taupe textures with brushed gold accents.
- **Typography**: Earthy, grounded, but sophisticated.

## 15 — SIGNATURE GOLD
- **Concept**: Pure materiality.
- **Visuals**: A full-screen texture of brushed champagne gold with shadows.
- **Typography**: Dark Espresso ink effect.
