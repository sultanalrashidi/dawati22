Aesthetic Description

"We are looking for something incredibly timeless and minimal, but with a touch of modern luxury. We love the idea of heavy cotton paper with a subtle texture, perhaps an off-white or ivory base. We want to avoid heavy embellishments, focusing instead on high-quality materials and perfect typography. A slight hint of gold foil on the main names would be perfect."
