# Dawati 2026 "Signature Collection" Invitation Specifications

This collection consists of 15 high-fidelity digital invitation templates. Each is designed as a standalone 9:16 mobile experience, intended to be sent via the Dawati app.

## Core Design Principles
- **Atmosphere over Assets**: Translate palace luxury into graphic shapes, lighting effects, and textures.
- **Typographic Centerpiece**: The couple's names (سارة & محمد) are the primary visual focus.
- **Dynamic Integration**: Every card has a dedicated, non-intrusive area for the Guest Name, Countdown, and QR Code.
- **Editorial Aesthetic**: High whitespace, sophisticated type scales, and premium material textures.

---

## The 15 Templates

### 01 — ROYAL ARCH
- **Visual**: A clean, vector-based architectural arch silhouette in brushed gold.
- **Palette**: Warm Ivory background.
- **Layout**: Names centered inside the arch.

### 02 — PALACE IVORY
- **Visual**: Subtle "stone" texture with ultra-thin 0.5px champagne divider lines.
- **Palette**: Ivory and Soft Cream.
- **Layout**: Asymmetric, editorial magazine style.

### 03 — MIDNIGHT PALACE
- **Visual**: Deep matte Charcoal background with a subtle "inner glow" around the names.
- **Palette**: Charcoal and Warm Ivory text.
- **Layout**: Dramatic, centered, high-contrast.

### 04 — CHAMPAGNE COUTURE
- **Visual**: High-fashion house aesthetic. Massive negative space. 
- **Palette**: Champagne Gold and Black.
- **Layout**: Names take up the top 40% of the screen.

### 05 — MODERN SAUDI
- **Visual**: Geometric patterns inspired by modern Riyadh architecture (linear, sharp).
- **Palette**: Warm Beige and Charcoal.
- **Layout**: Structured and balanced.

### 06 — GRAND BALLROOM
- **Visual**: Abstract "bokeh" light effects and gradient "chime" lines.
- **Palette**: Ivory and glowing Gold.
- **Layout**: Ethereal and soft.

### 07 — BURGUNDY LUXURY
- **Visual**: A rich, textured Muted Burgundy background (velvet feel).
- **Palette**: Burgundy, Ivory, and Gold.
- **Layout**: Classic, centered elegance.

### 08 — ESPRESSO & IVORY
- **Visual**: Dark, polished stone/wood texture.
- **Palette**: Deep Espresso and Ivory.
- **Layout**: Clean, sharp, and masculine-leaning but elegant.

### 09 — SCULPTURAL MINIMAL
- **Visual**: A single, bold 3D-rendered geometric "S" or "M" monogram shape.
- **Palette**: Stone and Gold.
- **Layout**: Radical minimalism.

### 10 — SOFT COUTURE
- **Visual**: Layered, semi-transparent panels with soft drop shadows.
- **Palette**: Dusty Rose and Ivory.
- **Layout**: Soft, feminine, and sophisticated.

### 11 — MARBLE EDITORIAL
- **Visual**: A high-res, subtle white marble background with gray veins.
- **Palette**: White, Gray, and Charcoal.
- **Layout**: Oversized, bold Arabic display typography.

### 12 — BLACK TIE
- **Visual**: Pure monochromatic elegance. No gold. 
- **Palette**: Jet Black and Crisp Ivory.
- **Layout**: High-contrast, sharp lines.

### 13 — GRAND ENTRANCE
- **Visual**: Two vertical "pillars" of light framing the content.
- **Palette**: Warm Stone and Champagne.
- **Layout**: Symmetrical and grand.

### 14 — MODERN PALACE
- **Visual**: Linear architectural lines forming a subtle grid.
- **Palette**: Beige and Taupe.
- **Layout**: Contemporary and architecturally precise.

### 15 — SIGNATURE DAWATI
- **Visual**: A blend of fine-line gold work and massive negative space.
- **Palette**: The Dawati Core Palette (Ivory/Gold).
- **Layout**: The definitive luxury standard.
