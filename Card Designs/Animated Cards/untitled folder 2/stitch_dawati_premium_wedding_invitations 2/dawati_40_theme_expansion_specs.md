# Dawati 40-Theme Expansion: Design Strategy & Categorization

This document outlines the visual directions for the 40 new interactive invitation themes, expanding the Dawati collection into a comprehensive, world-class design library.

## 1. Minimalist Series (10 Themes)
Focus on extreme refinement, negative space, and typographic authority.
- **Pure Ivory**: Zero decoration. Perfection through spacing and the Playfair Display names.
- **The Line**: A single 1px horizontal champagne line separating names from details.
- **Ghost Monogram**: Transparent, oversized 'S&M' letters as a structural background.
- **Architectural Grid**: Subtle gray grid lines inspired by blueprint precision.
- **Stone & Serif**: Light gray textured background (limestone) with charcoal serif type.
- **Centered Soul**: Absolute symmetry. Vertical line connects every element.
- **The Frame**: A 1px border with significant internal padding.
- **Floating Names**: Names take up 80% of the viewport height on the first scroll.
- **Paper Press**: Faux-embossed effect where names appear pressed into ivory cardstock.
- **Quiet Luxury**: Cashmere-colored background with tone-on-tone ivory text.

## 2. High Luxury & Royal (10 Themes)
Rich materials, dramatic lighting, and grand compositions.
- **Grand Ballroom**: Abstract golden bokeh and vertical 'light pillars'.
- **Velvet Midnight**: Deep navy velvet texture with glowing champagne text.
- **Palace Entrance**: Structural arch silhouette using soft shadows, not lines.
- **Pearl Luster**: Iridescent background with sharp, high-contrast black typography.
- **The Chandelier**: Crystalline refractions and light-play accents.
- **Silk Flow**: 3D-rendered silk folds in cream as the primary background.
- **Gilded Arch**: A modern, non-traditional arch with a subtle metallic glint.
- **Royal Espresso**: Polished dark wood texture with de-bossed gold lettering.
- **Majestic Arch**: Double-layered arch perspective for a sense of 'entering' the venue.
- **Crowned Typo**: Names sit above a very minimal, modern geometric crown symbol.

## 3. Saudi & Gulf Inspired (5 Themes)
Modern interpretations of regional heritage and architecture.
- **Modern Najdi**: Geometric triangular patterns used as subtle 'cut-outs' in the UI.
- **Riyadh Horizon**: Abstract linear representation of modern Saudi architecture.
- **Desert Rose**: Warm terracotta and taupe palette with sharp, modern Kufic accents.
- **The Gateway**: Modern geometric interpretation of a traditional wooden door.
- **Oasis Couture**: Muted olive and sand palette with a focus on organic, flowing lines.

## 4. Feminine & Romantic (5 Themes)
Soft palettes and delicate visual elements without being 'girly'.
- **Soft Couture**: Layers of semi-transparent taupe panels.
- **Botanical Fine-Line**: Single-line vector drawings of stems (no full flowers).
- **Dusty Rose Editorial**: Muted pink/brown palette with oversized fashion-house type.
- **Petal Shadows**: Soft, out-of-focus shadows of leaves falling across the card.
- **Romantic Arch**: Soft, curved architectural shapes in a warm cream palette.

## 5. Dark & Evening (5 Themes)
High contrast and sophisticated nighttime atmosphere.
- **Black Tie Pro**: Monochromatic black and white. Razor-sharp lines.
- **Midnight Palace**: Deep charcoal with a central warm light source 'behind' the names.
- **Burgundy Velvet**: Rich wine-red texture with ivory text and gold accents.
- **Smoked Glass**: Semi-transparent black overlays over a deep espresso background.
- **Eclipse**: A large, dark circle frames the names, creating a dramatic focal point.

## 6. Experimental & Modern (5 Themes)
Pushing the boundaries of the digital invitation format.
- **Asymmetric Edge**: Radical layout where names are pinned to the bottom-left corner.
- **The Reveal**: Content is hidden behind vertical 'panels' that slide as you scroll.
- **Geometric Play**: Bold, large-scale circles and squares in ivory and taupe.
- **Typographic Hero**: The names are rotated 90 degrees for a fashion-magazine feel.
- **Digital Horizon**: A minimalist, horizontal-focused layout with wide letter-spacing.
